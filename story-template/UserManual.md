# User Manual

Welcome to Coose. You play one character, and you only ever see the world through their eyes.

## Prompt types
- **Action `!`** Something your character does or says. The AI decides what happens. To speak, write it as an Action, for example: *I tell the guard, "I'm only passing through."* The people around you answer in the narration, and anyone nearby who notices hears it.
- **Question `?`** An out-of-character question, answered only with what your character would know.

## Time and rounds
The world has one clock. Actions take time (a few words take next to none); Questions don't. An Action that takes a long time (sleeping, travelling) becomes a **time skip**: your character is busy until the next round, when the clock jumps ahead. You can still ask Questions while you wait.

A **round** comes after a set number of Actions, or sooner if everyone online votes for it. At each round you get a short notice of what your character noticed changing.

## People
The panel beside your chat lists who is **here with you**. The narrator adds people who matter to that list as they come into the story, even before anyone knows their name ("Front gate guard"). If someone you can see is missing, ask a Question such as *"Can you add the guards to the people here?"*

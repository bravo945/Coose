# User Manual

Welcome to Coose. You play one character, and you only ever see the world through their eyes.

## Prompt types
- **Action `!`** Something your character does. The AI decides what happens.
- **Dialogue `""`** Something your character says to others at the same place. Tick who you are speaking to, or nobody to speak to everyone there. If their player is away, the narrator may answer for them, and they'll see what was said.
- **Question `?`** An out-of-character question, answered only with what your character would know.
